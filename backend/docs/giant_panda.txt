Giant Panda

Sources: This fact sheet was summarized from https://www.nationalzoo.si.edu/node/8281 (Smithsonian's National Zoo) and https://www.ifaw.org/animals/giant-pandas (IFAW).

Animal class: The giant panda is a mammal and a member of the bear family.

Birth: Giant pandas give birth to live young. A newborn giant panda cub is blind, very small, and almost hairless, and weighs only about 3 to 5 ounces.

Diet: Giant pandas eat almost nothing but bamboo in the wild, so they act like herbivores. A giant panda eats about 12 kilograms (about 26 pounds) of bamboo a day and spends 10 to 12 hours a day eating.

Habitat: Giant pandas live in cool, high-altitude bamboo forests in the mountains of central China, mostly in Sichuan Province, with smaller groups in Gansu and Shaanxi Provinces. Chinese scientists estimate that about 1,864 giant pandas live in the wild.

Hibernation or migration: Unlike other bears, giant pandas do not hibernate. In winter, giant pandas move down to lower elevations and keep eating as usual.

Fun facts: Giant pandas chirp, bleat, and bark to communicate with each other. Red pandas share the name but are not closely related to giant pandas. Giant pandas handle cold winters easily but have a harder time with summer heat.
